How to Add Routine to Your Phone Calendar

Step-by-step guidance for iOS Safari and Android devices

Step 1
Tap "Download .ICS Calendar File" above

NeoReminder generates an RFC-compliant calendar schedule file containing all your classes and alarms.

Step 2
Confirm Download in Safari

Safari prompts "Do you want to download 'class_routine.ics'?" — Tap Download.

Step 3
Open the Downloaded File

Tap the Downloads blue circle icon in Safari's address bar (or open the Files app → Downloads) and tap class_routine.ics.

Step 4
Tap "Add All" in Apple Calendar

The native Apple Calendar sheet opens with the full schedule. Tap "Add All" at the top right corner.

Success! All classes, teacher initials, room numbers, and alarms are now active in your iPhone Calendar!
Step 1
Tap "Download .ICS Calendar File" above

The .ics timetable file with your chosen reminder duration will download to your device.

Step 2
Open the File from Downloads

Tap the download notification in your notification tray, or open your phone's Files / Downloads app and tap the file.

Step 3
Choose Your Calendar App

When asked "Open with", select Calendar (Samsung Calendar, Google Calendar, or device calendar).

Step 4
Tap "Save All" or "Import"

Confirm the import. Your device will automatically schedule repeating events with your selected reminder alarm notification.

Success! Your weekly routine and alarm reminders are now live on your Android calendar and widgets!
